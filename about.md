# Wave Progress Bar

Turns the progress bar at the top of the screen into the **wave gamemode**.

- Your **equipped wave icon** rides along the bar, using your icon colors and glow.
- It leaves a **zigzag trail** at 45 degrees, just like a real wave.
- Choose between a clean **Classic** zigzag or an uneven **Chaotic** one.
- Adjust icon size, zigzag height, trail thickness, trail length and trail color in the mod settings.

Settings are applied when you enter a level. The "Show Progress Bar" option in GD's settings must be turned on.
